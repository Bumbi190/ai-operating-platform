## 1. Design intent

The Professional Edition should read as the more authoritative, higher-stakes sibling of the
Blueprint: same family, greater gravity. It must feel premium, dark, futuristic, strategic,
calm, authoritative, technically credible, and legible across hundreds of pages. It must not
feel like a Word report, a slide export, a marketing brochure, a neon dashboard, or an
over-designed template.

The premium and executive character is created by **dark feature surfaces** (cover, part
dividers, chapter openings, selected full-page diagrams, and very short doctrinal
statements) set against **light body pages** for all sustained reading. Dark body-text pages
for long-form reading are prohibited.

## 2. Series DNA preserved from the Blueprint

Retained for clear kinship: Letter page format and a top-weighted margin system; a
serif-based typographic foundation; the Blueprint Navy + Executive accent color base;
petrol table headers with light zebra rows; a running header carrying the series identity and
the chapter track; a centered "Page X of Y" footer; a small-caps chapter eyebrow; airy part
dividers with generous whitespace; a restrained callout aesthetic; and a generous
whitespace rhythm.

## 3. Professional Edition differentiation

Not copied directly from the Blueprint: the fully light identity (this edition is darker on
feature surfaces), the hard-coded table of contents (this edition uses a live TOC with PDF
bookmarks and internal links for a large book), the table-as-diagram approach (this edition
uses true reproducible vector diagrams), and the Blueprint's short 50-page scale (this
edition requires a full multi-part navigation system).

## 4. Dark / light hybrid (LOCKED)

Dark surfaces are used for: cover, part dividers, chapter openings, selected full-page
diagrams, and very short doctrinal statements. Light surfaces are used for: running body
text, long chapters, tables, lists, dense policies, longer examples, and validation or
reference material. No dark body-text pages are produced for sustained reading.

## 5. Non-canonical navigational grouping

The Professional Edition groups the 32 canonical chapters into ten visual Parts for
navigation only.

> Non-canonical navigational grouping added only in the Professional Edition.

These Parts do not create new canonical chapters, do not change chapter numbers, do not
change chapter order, do not change section numbers, do not create new doctrine, and are not
written as if they were new architecture principles. See `EXECUTIVE_INTELLIGENCE_LAYOUT_SPEC.md`
for the full Part table.

## 7. Fidelity rule

All text presented as book text in any Professional Edition artifact is reproduced verbatim
from Canonical v1.0. No summaries, rewrites, shortening, merging that changes meaning, new
autonomy levels, new authority rules, new Stage 1 capabilities, changed project status, or
changed policy are introduced. Diagram labels are derived directly from canonical text and
cited by section number in the proof report.
