### Additional Authority Wording Requiring Human Review

These items were open after Phase 1.1 and are now resolved by Phase 1.2:

- Chapter 16, section 16.51: `Executive may grant autonomous operating rights only through an approved autonomy license.`
  - Why ambiguous: assigns the granting verb directly to Executive, which may conflict with the approved doctrine that authorized governance grants bounded autonomous rights through an approved autonomy license, following an Executive recommendation.
  - Recommended future formulation: `Authorized governance may grant autonomous operating rights only through an approved autonomy license, following an Executive recommendation.`
  - Phase 1.2 action: resolved by approved wording correction.
- Chapter 17, section 17.88: `Grant earned bounded autonomy.`
  - Why ambiguous: imperative wording omits the approving actor and may be read as an instruction without an explicit governance or human approval subject.
  - Recommended future formulation: `Authorized governance may grant earned bounded autonomy through an approved autonomy license.`
  - Phase 1.2 action: resolved by approved wording correction.
- Chapter 32, section 32.212: `It should grant autonomy carefully.`
  - Why ambiguous: the antecedent is Executive Intelligence, which may imply that Executive Intelligence itself grants autonomy rather than recommending autonomy for approved governance action.
  - Recommended future formulation: `Authorized governance should grant autonomy carefully, based on Executive Intelligence recommendations and an approved autonomy license.`
  - Phase 1.2 action: resolved by approved wording correction.
