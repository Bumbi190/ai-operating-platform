# Demo Motors Luxury Automotive Footer — Design

## Intent
Upgrade only the Demo Motors footer so the page ends like an exclusive automotive website rather than a SaaS/product widget.

## Goals
- Remove the separate right-side CTA card.
- Use a wide, calm luxury composition with strong whitespace.
- Keep Handlarbörsen Demo Motors branding prominent.
- Keep two restrained link/info groups.
- Finish with one large editorial statement plus a small integrated gold action link.
- Preserve the existing dark showroom palette and warm gold accent.
- Preserve all existing page content, vehicle interactions, demo disclosures and no-send behavior.

## Non-goals
- No new forms, external links, model calls, images, 3D, publication or customer outreach.
- No changes outside the footer.
- No additional paid work.

## Selected approach
Luxury automotive footer: brand and navigation remain structured, while the conversion message becomes part of the footer typography rather than a bordered card. Premium comes from proportion, whitespace, hierarchy and restrained accent use.

## Rejected approaches
- Editorial full-width-only footer: visually strong but less practical for dealer navigation.
- Glass/luxury CTA card: still reads too much like SaaS and repeats the problem visible in the current preview.

## Responsive behavior
Desktop: asymmetric wide grid with statement/action occupying the right half.
Tablet: two-column composition.
Mobile: single-column stack with statement and action kept visually prominent.

## Acceptance
The footer has no separate CTA card/panel, remains keyboard-accessible, retains demo transparency, and the premium template compiles within existing artifact limits.
